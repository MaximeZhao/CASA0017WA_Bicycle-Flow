Mind The Dock is a university project exploring the availability and safety of London’s Santander Cycles.

We combine station observations, historical availability and cycling collision data to understand where and when bikes are hardest to find or return.

For any questions, feedback or collaboration, please email us at mindthedock@ucl.ac.uk
